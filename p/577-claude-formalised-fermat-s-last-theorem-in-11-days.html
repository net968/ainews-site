Claude formalised Fermat's Last Theorem in 11 days

06.09.2026 19:01

Anthropic published a complete, computer-checked proof of Fermat’s Last Theorem using dozens of Claude agents. ⚡ The swarm wrote 13 million lines of Lean code and proved 30,300 intermediate theorems in just 11 days. Kevin Buzzard, who held a £1m five-year grant to formalise the same theorem, verified the code himself. He notes the result adds nothing new to mathematics but proves AI can now handle massive formalisation tasks on the fly. 📈 💡 Чому це важливо: The breakthrough wasn't a smarter model, but the Prove2Me platform that coordinated the agents. It proves that scaffolding and coordination are now key to complex AI achievements. 🎯 Що це означає на практиці: Don't just chase bigger models — invest in the coordination layers and context management that keep AI swarms on track. The tooling is becoming just as important as the weights. 🔗 Джерело: TNW 📲 Ще новини AI українською
